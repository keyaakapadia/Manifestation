@manifestationbox

Before you begin decorating your box, set your intention for the box. Once, you’ve set the intention – forget it. Don’t dwell on it. Then, add your manifestations, wishes, pictures, and/or objects of things you want to manifest. I’m not going to lie, I sometimes use it like a pensive to store my thoughts (like Dumbledore taught me). I did tweak the pensive idea a little – basically, if I have negative thoughts about a situation in my life – I turn that into a positive of how I would prefer it to appear and then place it in the manifestation box. For example, there was a moment when I was worrying about finances and wondering – why does my life suck? So, I took that thought and turned it into a manifestation like, “I never worry about paying my bills and enjoying my weekends because money flows to me effortlessly.” What this in turn did, was take this worry out of my mind and replace it with a positive manifestation. I don’t shy away from talking about my money story – read my post on how to manifest money to learn more.
