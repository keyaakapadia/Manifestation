@369method

This method involves writing three affirmations, six times a day for nine days. You may choose to focus on a specific intention or desire and use that in lieu of an affirmation, but I prefer to create and use an affirmation for my desire. There isn’t a set number of days to continue this practice, but anywhere from 21 – 40 days is a good start. It’s important to preface that manifestation techniques should be used with actionable steps.
