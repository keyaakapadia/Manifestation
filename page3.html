@visionboard

You can create a virtual vision board for your phone or desktop and a physical one on a bulletin board. Personally, I have a vision board as my phone lock screen with high-level goals for the year + I have a bulletin board of both short and long-term dreams. Creating a vision board is a fun activity to keep you focused on the prize. It’s a good idea to tackle one area of your life at a time when you pick images for your vision board. For example – in regards to my health; I want to eat more veggies, I want to go for daily walks, and I want to stretch regularly. Once, I complete brainstorming ideas of how to take better care of my health – I look for images to match that and then I move on to another area of my life.
