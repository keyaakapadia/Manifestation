Can you tell us a bit more about it?

@placematmethod

Draw a line down the middle of a paper, then on one side write a to-do list of tasks that need to be completed today, and on the other side, write down things for the universe, that need to be completed but – don’t require immediate attention. Take a picture of your personal to-do list (not the universe side). Then, throw away the list. Go about your day, complete your tasks, and forget about the universe’s to-do list.

@placematmethod

When you write things you need down, you’re letting the universe know – I need you to do those things for me and that you can take care of the rest. By doing this your allowing yourself to receive from the universe. Let’s say I want to create a place mat list, my personal list might look like, “take Dobby for a walk”, “prep the bins for garbage day”, and “write a paragraph for a new post”. While, the universe’s list may contain, “finish a blog post”, “meal prep for the week” and “plan our summer trip”. Next, I’d keep a memory of the tasks at hand and discard the rest. Essentially, you are asking the universe for assistance by setting and forgetting.
