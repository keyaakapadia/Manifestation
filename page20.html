@emotionalfreedomtechnique

This manifestation technique lets you release negative energy around beliefs you have about yourself. Start by sitting down, rate your belief from 0-10, then begin tapping at meridian points in your body and think of the negative ideas or statements around that belief. After a few minutes, stop and rate the intensity of that belief again – if it still feels intense then keep going. Once, the intensity has dropped down to zero start tapping with positive beliefs to replace the negative ones.
