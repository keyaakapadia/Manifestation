This website is designed to guide you through the different types of manifestation—what they are, how they work, and how to integrate them into your daily life, making manifestation a habit as natural as checking your feed.

@lawsofattraction

The law of attraction is the belief that positive thoughts attract positivity in your life and vice versa for negative thoughts.

@visualization

Visualization is a manifestation technique used to visualize an image of what you want to attract. You can create a mental image in your mind or you can use an image you’ve sourced to help you with this exercise.

@visionboard

While on the topic of visualization – why not create a vision board? A Vision board is where you can store all sorts of images and phrases of what you want to attract into your life.

@affirmations

We love a good affirmation! Affirmations are positive statements that can be repeated daily to help shift your mindset. There isn’t one right way to use affirmations.

@gratitude

Just as the sun needs the rain – we need gratitude. Gratitude is a manifestation technique that asks one thing of you. That one thing is to practice the powerful act of being grateful for your life.

@manifestationscripting

Scripting is a powerful tool to enhance your manifestation by writing out the desired outcome of your goals and dreams.

@lettinggo

Like Elsa caught in never-ending song – Let. It. Go. This technique involves the law of detachment – which is the practice of letting go of attachment to a certain desired outcome.

@whispermethod

The whisper method manifestation technique (by Sarah Perl, aka @hothighpriestess on TiktTok) involves getting someone to do something for you.

@369method

The 369 manifestation method is based on Nikola Tesla’s teachings in which he purposed that the numbers 3, 6, and 9 are the key to the universe.

@2cupmethod

The 2-cup method for manifesting involves writing down your current reality and your desired reality then tape each reality to a cup with water (half-full) in it. Then pick up your current reality glass, hold it, and feel right into it.

@pillowmethod

For the pillow method, take a piece of paper, write your desire on it, read it out loud, and place it under your pillow.

@placematmethod

The place mat process by Abraham Hicks involves using the law of attraction to do less, yet attract more.

@manifestationbox

As the Law of attraction states – energy goes, where energy flows. So, write down and create a place to house your thoughts, goals, and ambitions to let that energy flow. Simply put, keep the things that you want to manifest in a manifestation box and sprinkle it with good intentions.

@lawofassumption

The law of assumption is the practice of assuming, that what you want to happen will happen.

@meditation

In your meditation – visualize everything you want in life, what you want to be in life, settle into the emotions behind it, and breathe deeply. By stepping into a meditation – you are allowing ease into your life and welcoming ideas to flow freely.

@journaling

Journaling is one of the most helpful ways to manifest. Essentially, you’re writing things into existence.

@raiseyourvibration

Raise your vibration via your self-love journey. A self-love journey is when you decide to stop, drop everything and roll into prioritizing yourself.

@soundhealing

Sound healing, also known as frequency therapy is a manifestation technique that is often used to heal your body.

@hypnotherapy

Hypnotherapy is a technique that allows you to pass your ego and speak to your subconscious mind. It is typically performed by a licensed healthcare provider.

@emotionalfreedomtechnique

The emotional freedom technique is often referred to as tapping and is used to neutralize negative energy blocks in your body by sending calming signals to your brain.

@subliminalmessaging

Subliminal messaging is audio recordings that contain hidden affirmations, vibrations, and/or frequencies which are created to be perceived by your subconscious mind.

@crystalhealing

In many cultures across the globe, crystals have long been known to provide direct benefits to your overall wellness. While there are tons of crystals, each with its own vibrational energy – not all of them will match your energy and needs.

@numerology

Numerology is a manifestation technique that can be used to manifest wealth and abundance. Numbers play a vital role for those that believe in the good fortune of numerology. Each number is meant to possess unique energy and represent different opportunities.

@fullmoon

A full moon or new moon ritual is a powerful manifestation tool that can help you set intentions, let go of the past and focus on achieving your goals.

@luckygirlsyndrome

In essence, lucky girl syndrome is based on the law of attraction. To find success with this trend, trust that the universe is rigged in your favor and everything is working out for you. You have to trust in your gut that something good is always happening to you. Remember – you’re the luckiest girl (person) in the world.

@workinsilence

Working in silence is the best way to protect your dreams from negative energy. I often equate negative energy from others with the evil eye and I’ve come to learn that even those with the best wishes, unintentionally might jinx you.

@actlikeyoualreadyhaveit

Acting like you already have it is by far one of the best ways to actually have it.
