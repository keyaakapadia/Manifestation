Learning to use manifestation techniques is the bread and butter of shifting your dreams into reality. The concept of manifestation is based on the law of attraction which dates back to many philosophical, cultural, and spiritual traditions. The law of attraction is the idea that like attracts like, suggesting that what we put into this universe – be it our thoughts, feelings, and behaviors – attracts the same. Energy matters.


Now, this doesn’t mean you can manifest yourself into a chair – for that, you’re going to have to look elsewhere (circle back to me – if it’s possible). It also doesn’t mean that we can control terrible circumstances and situations that just happen to us. After all, we can only manage the things in our control.


In this post, we’re going to double down on over 25 manifestation techniques that you can apply to your life – today! We’ll explore how effective some of these techniques are and how to use them to attract that dream life you want. I for one, have had quite a bit of luck with some of these techniques. If you met the 2019 version of me – you may have hit the ground running. Post-understanding and applying manifestation techniques (plus therapy, podcasts, and a lot of reading) version of me, is an entirely different human. So naturally, I want those successes and wins for you.


These manifestation techniques are great for beginners and veterans alike. Manifesting isn’t a one size fits all approach and often takes trying different techniques to truly get comfortable. So, let’s get into it!
