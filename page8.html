The whisper method manifestation technique (by Sarah Perl, aka @hothighpriestess on TiktTok) involves getting someone to do something for you.

@369method

Can you tell us a bit more about it?

@whispermethod

This could be a text back from a friend, a job interview, or a promotion that you’ve been working towards. To being this practice, close your eyes and envision yourself walking up to a spirit that embodies the person that you need something from and whisper your request in their ear three times. Feel it. See it. Once you open your eyes trust that this reality is already yours. Own it with complete conviction.
