@journaling

A few ways you can use journaling to manifest your life is by writing down what you are grateful for, creating a love letter to yourself, using scripting, weekly intentions, bullet journaling, and daily affirmations. You can use any format you like and aren’t required to stick to one thing but it’s an important habit to create when you’re trying to attract your desired life.
