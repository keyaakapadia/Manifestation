Can you tell us a bit more about it?

@lawsofassumption

This law allows you to attract, anything you desire into your life. We need to step into assuming that we are already living in our desired reality and embody that version of ourselves. When we deeply feel into that reality, we are letting it unfold for us. In turn, we create our reality. There are studies that speak to confirmation bias; that your brain looks for ways to prove your beliefs. So, we need to affirm and assume the desired version of ourselves for it to become our reality.
