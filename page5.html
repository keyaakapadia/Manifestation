Just as the sun needs the rain – we need gratitude. Gratitude is a manifestation technique that asks one thing of you. That one thing is to practice the powerful act of being grateful for your life.

Can you tell us a bit more about it?

@gratitude

The more and more you practice the act of being grateful – the more natural it becomes. When I began this practice, I struggled. A lot. I found so many wonderful things to be grateful for, but I was still on the fence about the greatness of my life. Fast forward, after many years of inner work – I feel grateful every day. Things like watching my dog fetch, walks with my husband, and, banter with my family is more than enough to make me truly feel every morsel of gratitude.

Can you tell us a bit more about how to practice it?

@gratitude

You can practice gratitude by writing down one thing that you are grateful for every day. It can be something like, “I had a nice shower today.” Eventually, you can increase your one-liner to a list of things you feel grateful to have in your life. My current practice comes to me through moments of joy that I experience on any given day. I find I catch myself in those moments and tell myself how grateful I am to have this life.
