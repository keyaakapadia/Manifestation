Can you tell us a bit more about it?

@manifestationscripting

The art of scripting involves writing a letter to the Universe or God (whoever you feel called to) and defining the life you want to live in the present or past tense. I prefer to use the past tense when scripting – while others may write in the present tense. When scripting in the past tense, you have a sense of trust that you have already achieved the goals that you seek. It’s important to cover every aspect from what you feel, what you see, and what exactly you’re doing in the reality that you want to achieve. Details are important. After you’ve completed the script – read it out loud to yourself and put it away. I prefer not to think about or look at the script. It’s a good idea to practice the law of detachment with script manifestation.
