Can you tell us a bit more about it?

@lettinggo

Instead of hoping, dwelling, and ruminating; you trust the universe or God to take the lead and provide you with the best possible outcome. By letting go you aren’t giving up, rather you are surrendering to the process. When I find myself caught up in how I wish things would be or could be – I fall back and surrender. I trust that my timing is always right.
