Can you tell us a bit more about it?

@soundhealing

Although there isn’t always science to back manifestation methods – people believe that sound healing can help you manifest your goals and desires by improving your mental and emotional state. The idea behind sound healing is that by using different vibrations and frequencies your body and mind embrace a therapeutic effect. There are various instruments such as singing bowls and gongs that can be used to achieve different frequencies. You can also listen to pre-recorded sounds online such as the popular Solfeggio frequencies which is a set of six-hertz frequencies that have unique healing properties.
